schoolcal privacy policy

schoolcal is a personal script used only by its owner for their own family.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar, with the owner's consent.
  • It reads emails from the children's schools to find dates and events, adds those events to a family calendar, and sends the owner's family a reminder email.
  • Email text is processed to extract events and is not stored, sold, or shared with anyone else.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
